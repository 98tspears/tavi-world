TAVI WORLD — LIFELONG LEARNING SANDBOX

This edition is a simulation research environment, not a survival game.
Tavi's simulated experiences can now permanently alter his learned dispositions,
action preferences, expectations, memories and later autonomous choices.

START ON WINDOWS
1. Extract the full ZIP before running anything.
2. Close any previous Tavi World PowerShell/launcher windows.
3. Double-click START-TAVI-WORLD.bat in the extracted folder.
4. Leave PowerShell open and visit http://localhost:8765/ in your browser.
5. Export your save under Profile > Settings before your first experiment.

RESEARCH
- Open Research > Conditions, adjust the six environmental inputs and start an experience.
- Watch Observations for time-series charts, decisions and event logging.
- Choose Keep experience & finish to permanently retain what Tavi learns.
- Choose Discard without learning for a control trial that does not alter Tavi.
- Profile > Development displays lifelong traits, learned expectations and preferences.
- Profile > Settings contains a checkpoint restore for the previous committed trial.

SAVE AND MODEL LIMITATIONS
- Primary key unchanged: tavi-world-windows-living-valley-v1
- Active experiences are staged separately until committed; interrupted sessions resume.
- Close/open simulation does not create true continuous background activity.
- Ollama remains optional; no live model was tested on this machine.
- Tavi's emotion and learning numbers are simulated states, not evidence of consciousness.


NEW VIEW CONTROLS: The map camera does not follow Tavi. Drag to pan, use + / − or the mouse wheel to zoom. On touchscreens, drag with one finger or pinch with two fingers. Select Find Tavi (or press F) to center on him once.

WIDE ZOOM UPDATE
- Zoom down to 30% (formerly 60%) using the minus button, wheel, pinch, or minus key.
- At 30%, the viewport covers twice the width and height compared with 60%.
- Camera remains independent; Find Tavi still centers it once.

OPTIONAL: Connect > Internet offers read-only research from selected public sites. Independent research is disabled until you enable it. This requires the Windows launcher and a live connection.
