TAVI WORLD — COGNITIVE COMMITMENTS EDITION

Close previous Tavi launcher windows. Extract this ZIP. Run START-TAVI-WORLD.bat.
Open http://localhost:8765/ in your existing browser to preserve your local save.
Export your save under Profile > Settings before upgrading.

Go to Records > Approvals to view the exact state of each project: pending permission, queued, traveling, working, blocked, or completed with evidence. Approved unfinished work does NOT require a new approval after an interruption. Blocked work can be retried; approved work can be cancelled with a recorded reason.

This version also adds a brain-inspired cognitive model (computational, not a human brain) and includes the improved Ollama HTTP 400 handling. It cannot guarantee conversation quality; the Windows Ollama model must be tested on your PC.

See README.md and docs/COMMITMENT-LIFECYCLE.md for details.
